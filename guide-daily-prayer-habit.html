Building a Daily Prayer Habit

Consistency in prayer isn't about perfection — it's about showing up, even briefly, every single day.

1. Anchor it to something you already do

Attach prayer to an existing habit — right after you wake up, before your first meal, or right before bed. This removes the need to "remember" and turns it into a natural trigger.

2. Start smaller than feels necessary

Two honest minutes daily will outlast twenty minutes you only manage twice a week. Build the habit first; depth grows naturally afterward.

3. Keep a simple prayer journal

Write one line a day — what you prayed for, or what stood out. Over weeks, this becomes a record of growth you can look back on.

4. Expect dry seasons

Not every prayer will feel powerful. Some days it's just discipline, not emotion — and that's still valid and valuable.
