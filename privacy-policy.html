Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Healingzion Management Ventures ("HMV", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through healingzion-website.pages.dev, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

We also automatically collect basic technical information when you visit the site, such as the page viewed, approximate location (city/country level), and time of visit, for analytics purposes.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to consultation requests and provide the services you request

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Paystack, to process payments securely

4. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or unsubscribe from update notifications at any time, by contacting us using the details below.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
